About

I am Huaiyu Wu, an M.S. student (Class of 2026) at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Shaobo Cui. I received my bachelor's degrees from Tsinghua University, where I completed a dual-degree program in Mathematical & Physical Basic Sciences and Engineering Physics. My research interests centre on world models and causal reasoning.

I am drawn to two questions. First, how a model can learn the dynamics of an environment from observations and turn them into predictions that are actually useful. Second, how interventions and counterfactuals can move a model beyond correlation toward causal structure that is transferable and interpretable. I am always happy to discuss and collaborate.
